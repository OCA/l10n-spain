**On the product page:**

1. Products with insurance enabled show a checkbox: *"Add Caser insurance protection (+5.50€)"*
2. Insurance price is displayed dynamically based on product price
3. When checkbox is marked and product added to cart, insurance line is created automatically

**In the shopping cart:**

4. Insurance lines appear as separate items with a blue *"Insurance"* badge
5. Insurance quantity matches the insured product quantity

**At checkout (payment page):**

6. A modal *"Protect Your Products"* appears automatically if there are uninsured products
7. The modal displays:
   - Product image and name
   - Insurance price per unit and total
   - Toggle switches to select which products to insure
8. Upon confirmation, page reloads with new insurance lines added
