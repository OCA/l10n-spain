To use this module:

#. Go to *Invoicing / Accounting > Reporting > Generic Statements > Supplier
   Payment Period*.
#. Select the company and the fiscal year.
#. Select the method that applies to the company's annual accounts.
#. For the normal method, select the legal payment days and the date used as
   the start of the period for each invoice.
#. Press *Compute*.
#. Review the figures. With the normal method, review the paid and pending
   operations in the detail, open them with *View Details*, or export the
   report to XLSX.
